# Editing the Premium Management Group website

**Where:** https://premiummg.sanity.studio
Sign in with the account you were invited with. After you publish something, the website updates by itself in **1 to 2 minutes**.

## The areas (left menu)

- **Pages and wording:** every page of the website, with all its text in English and French.
- **Blog articles:** the news and posts.
- **Project photos:** the photo gallery on the Projects page.
- **Site settings:** the company's social media links.

## Edit the text of a page

1. Open **Pages and wording** and pick a page: Home, About, Contact, Header and footer, Search engines, and so on.
2. Each page is split into sections (Hero, Stats, Footer...). Click a section to open it. Every line is shown in **English** and **Français**, side by side, already filled in with what the site says today.
3. Change the text and click **Publish**.

Things to know:
- **Keep the `{name}` parts.** Some texts contain a word in curly braces, such as `{name}`. The website replaces it with a service name or a number. Leave it exactly as it is, in both languages. The Studio warns you if it is missing.
- **Lists have a fixed number of items.** The page is designed for, say, exactly 3 reasons or 4 reviews. Edit the items, but do not add or remove any.
- **If a field is empty,** the site keeps its built-in text. Nothing breaks.

## Service pages, team, certifications and legal pages

Under **Pages and wording** you also find:
- **Service pages:** one per service (Caulking, Firestopping...). Here you can change the headline, the introduction, each card, the FAQ and the picture. You can add or remove cards and FAQ questions. A section with no items is hidden.
- **Team (About page):** groups and people, with an optional portrait. Without a portrait, the person shows their initials.
- **Certifications and suppliers:** the four accreditations and the supplier names.
- **Legal pages:** Terms and Privacy. Each section can have its text, written in English and in French (same parts in both). Sections without text show a "to be added" notice.

## Change pictures

**Site pictures** holds the photos that are not part of an article or a project: the home page picture, the facade and work photos, Denis's portrait, the four certification logos and the film on the home page.
- **Leave a field empty to keep the current picture.** Upload one only to replace it.
- The film must be an **MP4 with no sound, under about 3 MB**. A heavy file makes the home page slow.
- Use wide (landscape) photos, except portraits.

## Publish a blog article

1. Open **Blog articles** and click the **+** button.
2. Fill in the fields. **English and French are both required** for every text.

| Field | What to put |
|---|---|
| **Title** | The headline, in both languages. |
| **Web address** | Click **Generate**. Do not change it after the article is published, or old links will break. |
| **Category** | Pick the closest one (Company is for news about the business). |
| **Service this article leads to** | Optional. Leave it empty if the article is not about a service. |
| **Summary** | Two short sentences. It shows on the blog list and in Google. |
| **Cover photo** | A horizontal photo. Write what is in it in **Photo description** (for people who cannot see it). |
| **Minutes to read** | An estimate. |

3. Write the article in the **English** tab and the **Français** tab. Use the style menu: **Paragraph**, **Heading**, **Quote**, **Key takeaways box**. You can also add a **Bullet list**, a **YouTube video** (paste the video address) and a **Link button** (text and an https:// address).
4. **Both languages need the same parts in the same order.** If English has a heading and two paragraphs, French needs a heading and two paragraphs. A red icon appears if they differ. Hover over it to see what to fix.
5. Click **Publish** (bottom right).

## Add project photos

**Project photos**, then **+**. Add the photo, a short description, the category and, if you want, the project name (both languages, or neither). To change the order, use the optional **Order** number: smaller numbers come first.

## Social media links

**Site settings**: add each network and its full address (starting with https://). They show as icons in the website footer.

## Good to know

- **Draft vs. published:** nothing is visible on the website until you click **Publish**. Edits made afterwards also need **Publish**.
- **Hide an article:** open it, click the **...** menu, then **Unpublish**. **Delete** removes it for good.
- **Do not use the long dash** in texts: use a comma, a colon or a full stop.
- **Pasting text:** you can paste several paragraphs at once; they split into separate paragraphs by themselves.
- **Never share** your login or any link or password you are given for the site.

**Not in the Studio:** the menus and page layout, the logos in the header, and the order of the sections on a page. For those, ask the developer.
